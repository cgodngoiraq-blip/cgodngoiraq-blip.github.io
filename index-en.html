About & Institutional Governance

Established under Iraqi NGO Law No. 12 of 2010, CGOD adheres strictly to rigorous international standards of financial integrity and good governance. Our developmental programs are overseen by specialized operational committees reporting to the Executive Board in alignment with the General Secretariat of the Council of Ministers.

Poverty Alleviation

Supporting vulnerable communities through dignified social support and sustainable economic empowerment models.

Quality Education

Conducting professional workshops and skill-development initiatives for medical students, youth, and graduates.

Enhancing healthcare infrastructure and public health programs in partnership with medical colleges and hospitals.

Sustainable Growth

Fostering innovation, youth entrepreneurship, and long-term socio-economic resilience.
