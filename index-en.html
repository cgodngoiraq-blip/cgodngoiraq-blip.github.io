About Us, Governance & Accreditations

The organization was established as an official national development entity operating under Iraq's NGO Law No. 12 of 2010. We are committed to good governance and financial and administrative transparency, through specialized committees chaired by the Board and under the follow-up of the General Secretariat of the Council of Ministers.

Institutional Policies & Commitments

We adhere to the following policies in all our activities, projects and partnerships. Click any policy to view its main provisions.

Anti-Fraud & Anti-Corruption
  • Zero tolerance for bribery, fraud and favoritism.
  • Accepting or offering any gift or benefit in exchange for a decision or service is prohibited.
  • Any suspicion is reported to the Administrative Body and investigated confidentially.
  • Proven violations are referred to the competent legal authorities.
Child Protection
  • A child's safety and dignity come before every other consideration in any activity.
  • No worker or volunteer is left alone with a child during activities.
  • Children are not photographed, and their images or data are not published, without written consent from a parent or guardian.
  • All workers and volunteers sign a commitment to this policy before participating.
Prevention of Sexual Exploitation & Abuse (PSEA)
  • Absolute prohibition of any sexual exploitation, abuse or extortion of beneficiaries.
  • Assistance is free, and no beneficiary is asked for anything in return.
  • Sexual relationships between staff and beneficiaries are prohibited.
  • Good-faith reporters are protected from retaliation.
Conflict of Interest
  • Every member discloses any personal or family interest related to a decision.
  • The interested person does not take part in the discussion or vote on that decision.
  • The policy covers relatives up to the second degree.
  • Disclosures are recorded in the meeting minutes.
Procurement & Contracting
  • Purchases are based on documented need and best value for money.
  • At least three price quotations above the financial threshold set by the Administrative Body.
  • The selection decision is documented in writing and kept in the organization's records.
  • No dealings with entities in which a member has an interest without prior disclosure, or with sanctioned entities.
Financial Transparency
  • Funds are managed through a bank account in the organization's name, and all income and expenses are documented.
  • No cash is accepted without an official receipt.
  • The budget is presented to the General Assembly for approval.
  • An annual financial report and activity report are issued.
Code of Conduct
  • Integrity, respect and non-discrimination on grounds of religion, ethnicity or gender.
  • The organization's name and resources are not used for party political promotion.
  • Confidentiality of information and care of the organization's property.
  • Compliance with the bylaws and instructions of the Administrative Body.
Complaints & Reporting
  • Any beneficiary, volunteer or partner may submit a complaint or report through the published contact channels.
  • Complaints are handled confidentially and the complainant is informed of the outcome.
  • No retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.
  • Serious violations are referred to the competent authorities.

Full versions of the policies are available on request. For complaints and reporting: cgodngoiraq@gmail.com
