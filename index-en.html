About Us, Governance & Accreditations

The organization was established as an official national developmental entity operating under the Iraqi NGOs Law No. 12 of 2010. We adhere to the highest standards of good governance, financial, and administrative transparency through our specialized committees chaired by the Board of Directors under the supervision of the General Secretariat of the Council of Ministers.

Our Strategic Goals

Poverty Alleviation

Lifting vulnerable groups and needy families out of poverty through social solidarity and empowering development programs.

Quality Education

Conducting training workshops and seminars to develop youth and graduate skills and build professional capacities.

Health & Well-being

Enhancing healthcare services and medical awareness in coordination with academic and health institutions.

Sustainable Development

Developing capabilities and encouraging innovation and developmental solutions to support the national economy and youth.
